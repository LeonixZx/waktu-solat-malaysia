# Product direction · 2.0

Warm ivory `#FCF9F3`, amber `#A46A2A`, honey gold `#E8B86D` and dark charcoal typography. Original sunrise mosque artwork gives the home a visual focus. Quran and wisdom banners use separate original imagery. Native duotone icons and restrained rounded surfaces carry the language through every destination; night mode uses warm charcoal and gold.

The five main destinations are Home, Quran, Kiblat, Tasbih and Collections. The drawer also exposes schedules, calendar, zones, settings and source credits. All visible feature actions lead to working screens. Phone layouts scroll naturally; the tablet home pairs the hero and quick actions with prayer times and reading banners.

This version adds Quran reading/audio/bookmarks, offline devotional collections, a tasbih and daily tracker, calendar, monthly schedules and optional reminders/azan. The implementation is independently authored native Java and XML. The supplied mockups guide hierarchy and mood; their screens, artwork, branding and text are not reproduced.

Future work can include home-screen widgets, broader reviewed content, downloaded recitations and accessibility/localization expansion. Video streams and private publisher backends are outside the shipped scope.
